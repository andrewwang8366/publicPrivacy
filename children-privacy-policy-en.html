Children's Privacy Notice

Last updated: 6 September 2026

English · 简体中文

This notice applies to the children's learning apps that adopt it. We want children to be able to learn at ease, and we want guardians to understand clearly how information is handled.

1. We collect as little as possible

The App does not ask a child to create an account, and does not ask for a name, age, school, home address, phone number, email address or any other identifying information. The App does not build a child profile, and does not learn anything about a child through advertising or profiling.

2. Learning records stay on the device

Quiz progress, accuracy, favourites, stickers and settings are stored only on the device where the App is used, so that learning can be picked up again. The developer does not upload this content to a server, and does not sell it or give it to third parties.

3. Speech and sound

Some learning content uses the device's system speech capability, or plays audio that ships inside the App. Speech only handles the text that needs to be read out; it does not record a child's voice, and the App does not use the microphone for recording or recognition.

4. Advice for children

Please use the App with a guardian present. Do not enter your real name, phone number, home address, school, social media handles, photographs or any other information that could identify you. If a page outside the App asks for personal information, ask a guardian first.

5. Guardians' rights

A guardian may stop a child from using the App at any time, and may delete the learning records on the device by uninstalling the App or clearing its data. If a guardian believes a child has submitted personal information by mistake, contact us to request access, deletion or a stop to processing.

6. Recommendations and app store hand-offs

The App may offer recommendation entries for other learning apps. Tapping one hands over to the system or the app store; whether to install it and keep browsing is the guardian's decision. The App itself does not read any child information from app store pages.

7. Contact us

8. Changes to this notice

If how we handle information changes, we will update this page and its date, and tell guardians in an appropriate place.
