Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

English · 简体中文

Children's privacy notice: read the children's privacy notice.

This Privacy Policy applies to the offline learning apps that adopt it (each, "the App"). We take the privacy of our users, and of children in particular, seriously. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Information we collect

The App requires no registration and no sign-in, and does not actively collect your name, phone number, email address, precise location, contact list, photo library, contacts, device identifiers, or any other information that identifies an individual.

Learning progress, quiz results, favourites and sticker state are stored only on this device, so that progress can be restored and results displayed. They are never uploaded to a developer server.

2. Speech, sound effects and device permissions

The App may use the system speech capability and audio files that ship inside the app to read learning content aloud or to give feedback. The App does not record audio, does not build a voice profile, and does not ask for microphone permission.

The App does not use the camera, the photo library or location services. The exact permissions are those shown on the system's permission screen for the installed app.

3. Network and third-party services

The core learning content works offline. The App contains no advertising, no account system and no in-app purchases, and uses no third-party analytics, profiling or behavioural tracking services.

Recommendation entries in the App may ask the system app store to open the detail page of another app. That hand-off is handled by the system or the app store, and its privacy rules are governed by that platform's own policy.

4. Local storage and deletion

The App stores only the state it needs — completion progress, quiz records and settings — on the device itself. Deleting the App or clearing its data removes that local state. Because the App creates no online account, there is no account data to be deleted remotely.

5. Children's privacy

The App is designed for children's learning. It does not actively collect children's personal information, does not create a child profile, and does not provide children's personal information to third parties. We recommend that children use the App with a guardian present, and that names, contact details, addresses and similar information are not entered into any input field, feedback area or other location.

If a guardian believes a child has provided personal information to the App, please contact us using the details below and we will help once the request has been verified.

For details, see our children's privacy notice.

6. Security

We reduce privacy risk by collecting as little as possible and by keeping everything in local storage. Please look after your device and use the lock screen and security features the system provides.

7. Changes to this policy

If the App's features, its data handling or applicable law change, we will update this page and its date. Significant changes will be pointed out on the app's store page or inside the App.
